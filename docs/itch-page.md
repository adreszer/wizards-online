**Arcane Academy** to szkoła magii, w której uczysz się razem z innymi graczami. Jeden wielki zamek z błoniami, wieżami, lochami i ukrytymi komnatami czeka na odkrycie.

## Description (EN)

**Arcane Academy** is a school of magic you attend with other players. One large castle with grounds, towers, dungeons and hidden rooms waits to be explored.

- **Lessons are taught by real professors.** You learn spells in class, then use them around the castle: to open passages, solve puzzles and uncover secrets.
- **Four houses:** Drakoryn, Grypheon, Phoenara and Hipporys. The Choosing Stone sorts you into one. Earn points for your house and compete for the cup.
- **Secrets everywhere.** Hidden doors, clues spread through the castle, puzzles for several players at once.
- **School life:** a timetable with bells, meals in the Great Hall, homework, exams, clubs, duels only in the duelling club, broom flying.
- **Nearby chat and private messages**, moderated.
- **Offline mode** for exploring at your own pace and practising puzzles.

The game is in early access and grows with every update.
