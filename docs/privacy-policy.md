## English

**Controller:** [CONTROLLER]. Privacy contact: [CONTACT].

**What we process and why**

| Data | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A random device id (made by the game on first start) | Signing in to your character without a password | Until the account is deleted |
| Character name, looks, house, year, role, spells, items, gold, progress, grades, homework | Running the game: saving your character | Until the account is deleted |
| Email and password (only if you add them in Settings → Account) | Recovering the character on another computer; the password is stored only as a hash (bcrypt) | Until the account is deleted |
| Public and private chat messages (with character name, time and place in the castle) | Moderation: reviewing reports and protecting players, especially younger ones | 7 days, then deleted automatically |
| Player reports, mutes, kicks and blocks | Moderation | Until handled and as long as needed to protect other players |
| Daily aggregate counts (visits per place, how many students played that day), never who | Improving the game | 60 days |
| IP address and technical connection data in server logs | Security and fixing bugs | As kept by the server logs, no longer than [N] days |

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract (providing the game, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest (moderation, security, aggregate statistics; Art. 6(1)(f)).

**Where the data is:** on the game server at [HOST]. We do not sell data or pass it to advertisers. The game has no ads or trackers. The game is downloaded through itch.io, which has its own privacy policy.

**Children:** in Poland, people aged 16 and over can consent to online services themselves. Younger players need a parent's or guardian's consent. Chat is moderated: word filter, reports, muting and blocking.

**Your rights:** access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. You can delete your character yourself in the game (Settings → Account → Delete my character). This removes the account and everything stored with it. Chat messages expire on their own after 7 days. For anything else write to [CONTACT]. You may also complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

**Changes:** changes to this policy are announced on the game's page. Last change: [DATE].
